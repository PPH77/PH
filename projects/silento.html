Finding a café is easy. Finding one where you can actually focus is not.

Silento is a full-stack web platform designed to help users discover cafés suited for studying, remote work, and focused environments through map-based discovery and a dedicated quietness scoring system.

NEXT.JS ASP.NET POSTGIS GEMINI AI LEAFLET JWT

> MISSION BRIEF

The problem Silento was built to solve

THE PROBLEM

Cafés have become common spaces for studying, remote work, and informal meetings. However, finding a location that is actually suitable for concentration is often based on trial, error, or personal experience.

THE GAP

Traditional location platforms provide ratings, reviews, and navigation, but they are not specifically designed to evaluate quietness, focus, or work-friendly environments.

THE SILENTO APPROACH

Silento adds a dedicated layer of environmental evaluation to café discovery, allowing users to browse locations while considering their suitability for focused activities.

> SILENT SCORE

The system's defining mechanic

WHAT DOES IT MEASURE?

Traditional café ratings answer whether people generally like a place. Silento introduces another question:

"HOW SUITABLE IS THIS PLACE FOR FOCUS?"

The system processes available café reviews and extracts noise-related semantic signals such as quiet, calm, noisy, or crowded.

> BOSS FIGHTS

Problems encountered during development

01

LIMITED REVIEW DATA

Review availability varied between café branches, creating a risk that small datasets could produce unstable results.

→ RESPONSE: NORMALIZATION + STABILIZATION

02

EXTERNAL DEPENDENCIES

External services introduced dependencies around café information, reviews, AI processing, and communication.

→ RESPONSE: MODULAR SERVICE ARCHITECTURE

03

BALANCING THE SCORE

A single positive or negative review should not dominate the overall perception of a café.

→ RESPONSE: WEIGHTED SIGNALS + MIXED SENTIMENT
